Support - RFID PH

RFID PH shows your Autosweep and Easytrip toll balances together in one app, along with each account's recent tolls, reloads and statements, so you don't have to check two provider websites separately. This page covers common questions and how to reach us.

Does this app need my Autosweep or Easytrip password?

No. You only ever enter an account number, never a password for either provider.

How do I add an account?

Tap the + button on the Autosweep or Easytrip row of the Summary card, give it a nickname, enter its account number, and tap "Check & Save". The app looks up the current balance and saves it.

How do I refresh a balance?

Tap the refresh icon on any account card to check just that one, or pull down on the home screen to refresh everything at once. There's a short cooldown of about ten seconds after a successful refresh of the same account, so the provider's site isn't hit repeatedly in quick succession.

Why is a lookup sometimes slow, or says the server is busy?

Balances are fetched live from each provider's own site, so lookup speed depends on how quickly that provider responds. Autosweep in particular can occasionally take up to a minute, or report itself as busy - if that happens, waiting a bit and trying again usually works.

How do I see an account's tolls, reloads or statement?

Tap the account card, or the receipt icon on it. For Autosweep you'll see its Tolls and Reloads month by month - use the arrows to change month. For Easytrip, pick a month under Statement Period and tap "Generate Statement"; your name, address and tag details are covered on it, and the camera button at the top right saves or shares a copy. Both go back up to 6 months.

Why isn't a toll or reload I just made listed yet?

Tolls, reloads and statements come from each provider's own records, which update about a day behind: anything from today shows up tomorrow, and some tolls take a day or two more to be posted. Your balance is checked live whenever you refresh, so it may already include a toll or reload that isn't listed yet.

How do I change an account's nickname?

Open the account and tap its nickname at the top of the screen.

Can I remove an account?

Tap the delete icon on an account card to remove it entirely, along with everything the app saved for it.

Can I lock the app?

Yes. Turn on App Lock in Settings and the app will ask for your device's Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode when you open it. RFID PH doesn't create or store a PIN of its own - it uses your device's own authentication.

Is RFID PH affiliated with Autosweep or Easytrip?

No. RFID PH is an independent utility app and is not officially affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by either provider. Balances are retrieved from each provider's own customer portal and reflect what those portals report at the time of checking.
