Support - RFID PH

RFID PH shows your Autosweep and Easytrip balances in one app, with each account's tolls and reloads.

Do I need my Autosweep or Easytrip password?

No. You only enter an account number.

How do I add an account?

Tap + on the Autosweep or Easytrip row, enter a nickname and the account number, then tap "Check & Save".

How do I refresh a balance?

Tap the refresh icon on an account, or pull down on the home screen to refresh all. If a provider is busy, the app tells you when to try again.

Why is a check sometimes slow?

Balances come live from each provider, so it depends on how fast they respond. If it's busy, wait a bit and try again.

How do I see tolls and reloads?

Tap the account, or the receipt icon on it. Use the arrows to change month, up to 6 months back. Pull down to load a month again.

Why isn't a recent toll or reload listed?

Providers take time to post them. Autosweep shows them about a day later; Easytrip usually within minutes. Your balance may already include it.

Can I see my statement?

Yes. On the tolls and reloads page, tap SOA at the top. Enter the account's plate number to open it. If that doesn't work, try the conduction sticker, or for Autosweep, the card number. Tap Share to save or send it.

How do I change a nickname?

Open the account and tap its nickname at the top.

Can I remove an account?

Tap the delete icon on the account. Everything saved for it is removed.

Can I lock the app?

Yes. Turn on App Lock in Settings to use your phone's Face ID, Touch ID or passcode.

Is RFID PH part of Autosweep or Easytrip?

No. It is an independent app.
