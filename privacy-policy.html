Privacy Policy - RFID PH

Last updated: November 10, 2026

RFID PH ("the app") lets you check your Autosweep and Easytrip balances and see your tolls and reloads. This page explains what the app keeps and what it sends.

What is saved on your phone

  • The nicknames and account numbers you add
  • Each account's balance history, for up to 1 year
  • The tolls and reloads of months you have viewed, for up to 6 months
  • Statements of past months you have opened, for up to 6 months

All of this stays on your phone and is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

What is sent over the internet

To check a balance, tolls, reloads or a statement, the app sends the account number to Autosweep or Easytrip - nowhere else. We have no server, so none of it reaches us.

The app also downloads announcements and settings from our website, hosted on GitHub. These requests carry none of your data.

Statements

A statement shows the account holder's name and address, so the app asks for the account's plate number (or conduction sticker or card number) before opening it. The number you type is not saved. A statement is only shared if you tap Share.

Notifications

Low balance alerts are made on your phone. No outside service is used.

Purchases

Memberships are bought through the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only remembers which membership you have.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID to show and measure ads, including personalized ads. On iOS, the app asks before allowing tracking, and you can change this in Settings. On Android, you can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalization in Settings. See Google's privacy policy for more.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your name, email, phone number, location or any other personal information. There is no sign-up or login. We don't sell any data.

Third-party sharing

  • Companies behind an ad may receive data if you tap the ad or install what it advertises.
  • Advertising partners may learn which ad led you to install the app.

Deleting your data

Delete an account in the app to remove everything saved for it, or uninstall the app to remove everything. We hold nothing about you, so there is nothing to ask us to delete.
