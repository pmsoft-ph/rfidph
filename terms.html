What the app does

RFID PH lets you check and track toll RFID account balances for Autosweep and Easytrip by entering an account number. Balances are retrieved from each provider's own systems and shown to you. The app is an independent convenience tool - it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with Autosweep, Easytrip, or any toll operator.

Accuracy of balances

Balances shown in the app come from the providers and may be delayed, temporarily unavailable, or out of date - for example if a provider's system is down, or if you have reloaded very recently. The app cannot reload your account or change your balance. Always treat your provider's own app, website, or a toll booth as the authoritative source, and do not rely on RFID PH alone to decide whether you have enough balance to travel. We are not responsible for any toll charge, penalty, delay, or other loss arising from a balance that was inaccurate, stale, or unavailable.
